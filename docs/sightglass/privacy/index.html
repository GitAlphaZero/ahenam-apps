Sightglass Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026 · Published by Ahenam

Sightglass ("the app") is a calculator and reference tool. This policy explains what information is collected when you use it and how it is used.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts. Everything you enter into the calculators stays on your device. We do not receive it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers, including the Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when iOS asks
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions, app usage and diagnostic data (such as crashes and performance)

If you decline tracking, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized using the IDFA. Users in regions that require it are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and can change that choice later. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

If the app offers in-app purchases, they are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase is active, so paid features can be unlocked.

Children

The app is intended for tradespeople and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. We do not store personal data on our own servers. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's policies.

Your choices

  • Change tracking permission: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • Reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in iOS Settings

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
